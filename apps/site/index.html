Fish a sea nobody can see into.

Every square of the sea is encrypted on chain. You pick a depth and a bait, cast blind, and the contract decides what bites. Only you can read your catch, and your score stays sealed until you choose to open it.

What is sealed

  • Nobody knows what lives where.

    The sea has 64 squares, each with three depths: surface, middle and bottom. Fish are drawn from encrypted randomness on chain, so not the players, not the keeper and not the server can see them.

  • Your bait is sealed before it leaves the page.

    Your browser encrypts your depth and bait. The contract compares them without seeing either, and a miss costs the same gas as a catch.

  • Your score opens only with proof.

    Your total grows sealed. It opens with a Zama decryption proof, and the contract checks that proof before your name goes on the leaderboard.

  2. 2

    Cast

    Hook it when it takes the bait and keep the line from snapping, or leave the line out and cast again.

On the water

Sail a small boat around an island, anchor in a chart square and fish from the deck. Waves, light and sea grass run in WebGL2 on your phone or desktop.

Watch it bite

A window under the water shows the fish come in, peck, and take the bait when the float twitches.

Depth, bait and loot

Each fish lives at one depth and bites on certain baits. Empty casts sometimes bring up a chest.

Hidden nets

Leave a net in a square for an hour. If a rival lands a common or uncommon fish there, you score it three times. A net costs 400 coins, and you can have two.

The Leviathan

It hides at the bottom of one square. Live fish at the bottom there lands 100 points, then it moves.
