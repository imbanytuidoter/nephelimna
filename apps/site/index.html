What is sealed

The lake is encrypted. So is your luck.

  • Squares nobody can read

    Each of the 64 squares holds three depths of fish drawn from encrypted randomness on chain. Not the players, not the keeper, not our server knows what lives where.

  • Choices encrypted in your browser

    Your depth and bait are sealed before they leave the page. The contract compares them with the square without ever seeing either, and pays the same gas for a miss and a catch.

  • Scores opened with a proof

    Your total grows sealed. When you post it, Zama decrypts it with a proof that the contract checks before your name goes on the leaderboard.

A cast, step by step

From your thumb to the chain and back.

  1. Seal the bait

    The game encrypts your depth and bait for this contract and your session key.

    in the browser

  2. Cast on Sepolia

    One transaction. The contract picks the layer, checks the bait and updates your total, all on encrypted values.

    about 10 to 20 s

  3. Read your catch

    Only your key may decrypt the result. A fish swims to your hook in the window under the water.

    about 5 to 40 s

  4. Strike and reel

    Hook it when it takes the bait and keep the line from snapping. Leave the line out and cast again if you like.

    your skill

On the water

A real fishing game, not a form.

Sail a small boat around an island, anchor in a chart square and fish from the deck. Everything that decides the outcome happens under encryption.

Open water

Sail, anchor, cast

Take the helm, find a square on the chart, drop anchor and fish. Waves, light and sea grass run in WebGL2 on your phone or desktop.

Under the float

Watch it bite

A window under the water shows the fish come in, peck and take the bait the moment the float twitches.
